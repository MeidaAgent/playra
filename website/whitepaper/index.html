Executive Summary

Playra is a browser farming game focused on meaningful progression. Players grow crops, raise animals, fish, craft resources, complete quests, and build their farms over time.

Economy Principle: Build Stability Before Token Decisions
Playra is currently prioritizing a balanced, understandable in-game economy. PYRA is future consideration only: there is no confirmed launch date, contract, network, trading venue, allocation, or financial return.

Whitepaper > Future Economy Consideration

Future Economy Consideration

Playra's current economy consists of gameplay progression and in-game resources. PYRA is only a future consideration and has not launched.

Gameplay Resources

Seeds, tools, and upgrades are balanced as in-game progression resources, with no external asset requirement.

Stable Progression

Land and crafting progression will remain understandable and achievable through gameplay.

Tool Crafting

Crafting systems may consume gameplay materials and should provide meaningful utility without external payments.

No Active Reward Pool

No daily token pool, token distribution, staking reward, or external value distribution is active.

Whitepaper > Seasonal Gameplay

Seasonal Gameplay Model

Seasons provide shared goals, limited-time activities, leaderboards, and rotating cosmetic or gameplay content. No external token reward is implied:

1. Seasonal Objectives
Players complete farming, crafting, and event activities that support balanced progression and community participation.
2. Farming Points
Gameplay activity contributes to in-game season progress. Progress is recorded server-side for a fair experience.
3. Season Rewards
Available rewards will be listed before each season. Any external asset is outside the current scope and is never implied by participation.
4. Seasonal Review
The team evaluates season duration, difficulty, participation, and fairness before announcing subsequent seasons.
Whitepaper > Core Farming Loop

The Core Farming Loop

Instant Playability: Click to plant, wait for the growth timer, click to harvest. No complex blockchain transaction approvals needed for each click. Gameplay is fast, responsive, and casual-friendly.

1. Plant Seeds

Choose your crop strategy based on growth time, resource availability, and active session length.

2. Water & Care

Keep soil watered to maximize growth speed. Upgrade to Sprinklers for passive cultivation.

3. Harvest & Sell

Harvest mature crops and receive their gameplay resources, with applicable season objectives updated automatically.

4. Expand & Reinvest

Use earned gameplay resources to expand your farm, improve recipes, and unlock new areas.

Whitepaper > Livestock & Animal Husbandry

Livestock & Animal Husbandry

Feed your farm animals to produce valuable cooking ingredients and trade commodities:

Hen House (Chickens)

Feed chickens Wheat to harvest fresh eggs. Eggs are essential ingredients for bakery cakes, pastries, and pancake recipes.

Wooly Sheep Pen

Care for sheep to produce soft wool for island quests, deliveries, and expansion supplies.

Dairy Cows

Cows produce rich milk when properly fed. Milk powers gourmet recipes, cheese production, and high-tier deli items.

Honey Apiary

Place beehives near blooming flowers to gather pure honey for sweet bakery recipes and stamina boosts.

Whitepaper > Mining, Gathering & Land Expansions

Mining, Gathering & Land Expansions

Players start on a compact starter island. Crafting axes and pickaxes unlocks materials and opportunities to expand into new areas:

Forest Trees (Wood)

Chop trees with an Axe (recovers every 2 hours). Wood is required to construct bridges to new island expansions.

Mineral Quarries

Mine mineral clusters with a Pickaxe. Yields stone, iron, and gold crystal minerals for machine automation.

New Land Plots

Every bridge built unlocks 3-5 new soil plots, additional trees, and water springs, drastically increasing your daily token yield.

Coastal Piers

Build fishing docks to catch rare marine species and complete gameplay objectives.

Whitepaper > Future Economy Design

Future Economy Design

Economy-first approach:
Gameplay stability takes priority over speculative economy features. No public token, contract, burn mechanism, staking vault, or external reward system is currently active.

Progression Value

Production, crafting, upgrades, and seasonal objectives are designed around clear gameplay value rather than external financial incentives.

Resource Balance

Future resource sinks must preserve fair progression, avoid forced spending, and be useful within the game itself.

No Staking Program

There is no active staking, lock-up, yield, vault, or bonus program. No returns or financial benefits are promised.

Future Review

If an external economy is approved, network, contract, access, and security details will be published separately after implementation and independent review.

Whitepaper > Security, Fair Play & Token Governance

Security, Fair Play & Governance

Playra values economic fairness above all else. Automated bots and malicious scripts are prevented through cryptographic session verification and proof-of-gameplay state machines:

Proof of Gameplay

Every harvest and transaction is verified against server-side crop growth clocks to prevent automated speed hacks.

No Current Smart Contract Dependency

Routine gameplay does not require a wallet or smart contract. Any future on-chain integration would be disclosed independently and would not be part of current gameplay.

Multi-Sig Treasury

Community and development funds are secured in multi-signature Gnosis Safe vaults requiring multiple executive approvals.

Rate-Limited APIs

DDoS protection and signed nonce handshakes protect all backend endpoints from unauthorized extraction.

Whitepaper > Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Has the PYRA token been launched?

No. PYRA remains a future consideration without a confirmed launch date, contract address, network, allocation, or trading venue.

Can players earn tokens by playing today?

No. There is currently no token earning, withdrawal, staking, reward pool, or external asset redemption program.

Is a crypto wallet required to start playing?

No. Players can use supported account services and play in the browser without a wallet. Any future wallet feature will be optional unless stated otherwise.

Which blockchain network will PYRA use?

No network has been confirmed. Only announcements made through Playra's verified official channels should be considered authoritative.
