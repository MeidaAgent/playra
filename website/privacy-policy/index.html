Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Playra Ecosystem

Playra ("we", "us", or "our") understands that protecting personal information is important. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect and use when providing the Playra website and game ("Services").

1. The Information We Collect

Personal Information is information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable. The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Account & Player Data: Supported wallet or account identifier when supplied, X handle, email, and in-game username.
  • Interaction Data: Farm progress, items crafted, completed activities, preferences, and gameplay or marketplace activity where those systems are enabled.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser session metadata, anonymized IP address, operating system, and non-identifying telemetry for bot prevention via Cloudflare Turnstile.
  • Community Communications: Support requests, bug reports, and survey feedback submitted through official channels.

2. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information in several ways, including:

  • Directly from you when you connect an account, join a waitlist, create a profile, or play the game;
  • Automatically as you navigate the website, through non-invasive cookies and telemetry;
  • Public information associated with a blockchain account only where a player-facing blockchain feature is enabled;
  • Third-party verification tools used solely for anti-sybil and DDoS mitigation.

3. Purpose of Collection, Use, and Disclosure

We collect and use personal information for purposes including:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Playra platform;
  • To verify supported account connections and protect the integrity of gameplay systems;
  • To detect and prevent bots, cheating, multi-accounting, and fraudulent exploit attempts;
  • To communicate seasonal updates, chapter quest drops, and leaderboard rewards;
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations where required by law.

4. Future Blockchain Features

Playra does not currently require blockchain transactions for normal gameplay. If a blockchain feature is introduced in the future, additional and specific disclosures will identify the network, asset type, transaction behavior, and publicly visible information before participation.

5. Security of Information

We are committed to ensuring that the information we hold is secure. We implement industry-standard organizational and technological safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. We will never ask for your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of off-chain personal data held by us. To submit a request, contact us via our official community support desk.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us:

  • Official Inquiries: Playra on X
  • Website: https://www.playra.io
