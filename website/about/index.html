ABOUT THE PROJECT

The Token-Powered Web3 Farming Game

Playra is an open-source, decentralized farming simulation game where players plant crops, harvest bounties, raise livestock, and participate in a fair, token-driven agricultural economy.

Our Mission

Playra was created to build a fun, transparent, and sustainable gaming ecosystem where players truly control their time and digital assets. Unlike speculative crypto games filled with pay-to-win mechanics and expensive speculative gates, Playra is free to start for everyone, powered purely by the $PYRA utility token.

Pure Token Economy

All gameplay rewards, trading, and progression revolve around the $PYRA utility token with zero speculative paywalls.

Verified On-Chain

All core smart contracts are deployed with publicly verifiable bytecode and transparent on-chain mechanics.

Built on Robinhood

Leverages Robinhood's high-speed blockchain infrastructure for seamless, instant micro-transactions.

Pioneer Community

A passionate growing community of early adopters and farmers preparing for the official launch.
